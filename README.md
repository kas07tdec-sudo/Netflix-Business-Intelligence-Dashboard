Netflix Business Intelligence & Data Analysis InternshipA comprehensive data analytics project completed as part of the 4-week Data Analysis Internship Program with Auspify Technologies. This repository contains Python scripts, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualizations, and an executive business intelligence report analyzing the global Netflix content catalog.
📊 Project OverviewThis project evaluates an extensive dataset of Netflix titles ($\approx 8,790$ records) to uncover actionable business insights regarding content composition, geographic production hubs, historical release trends, and audience demographic preferences.
Core Tasks Completed:Data Cleaning & Preprocessing: Handled missing values, standardized formats, and validated record integrity.Content Type Distribution: Analyzed the proportion of Movies versus TV Shows in the catalog.Geographical Production Analysis: Identified top content-producing countries (led by the United States and India).Release Year Trend Analysis: Tracked historical production scaling, identifying the 2018 peak production year.Rating & Genre Segmentation: Evaluated mature audience classifications (TV-MA, TV-14) and top engagement genres (International Movies, Dramas).Executive Business Intelligence Report: Synthesized all findings into structured strategic recommendations for executive stakeholders.
📈 Key Findings & InsightsCatalog Composition: Movies constitute the majority of the platform at 69.69%, while TV Shows account for 30.31%.Top Producers: The United States (3,240 titles) and India (1,057 titles) serve as the primary global media hubs.Production Peak: Content production experienced exponential growth starting around 2014–2015, peaking in 2018 with 1,146 titles released.
